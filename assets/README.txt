Put your app images here:

  icon.png            1024x1024   app icon
  adaptive-icon.png   1024x1024   Android foreground (safe zone ~66% center)
  splash.png          ~1284x2778  launch screen (transparent/centered mark works well)

Until you add them, remove the icon/splash/adaptiveIcon lines from app.json
to fall back to Expo defaults — the app will still build fine.
